Try it: tap the filled triangles to adjust weight by 2.5 kg or reps by 1. Tap a number to type instead. The small graphics have larger, separate tap areas. Each alternative has independent demo data; reload to reset.

The workout screens use a 256-pixel canvas, the approved steel frame, the home artwork’s material colors, and true lowercase Pixel Operator glyphs. Screens scale to whole device pixels. The explanatory page is outside the NES screen.

These are isolated interaction mockups; they never open your workout database. The DW3 capture linked in the vision was unavailable from this environment. The study follows its documented short-menu direction; direct capture comparison remains open.
